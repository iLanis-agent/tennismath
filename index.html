🎾 TennisMath

Winning 51% of the points feels like a coin flip. It isn't. TennisMath runs the real scoring math - games, deuce, tiebreaks with the actual serve pattern, sets, matches - and shows how a whisper-thin edge on each point becomes a landslide over five sets.

  • Hold of serve - point-win% to game-win%, with deuce solved exactly (65% of serve points is an 83% hold, ask any pro).
  • Tiebreak odds - the real 1-2-2 serve pattern, not a coin-flip approximation.
  • Set and match odds - hold and break rates compounding through six games and a tiebreak, then best-of-3 or best-of-5.
  • The edge table - 51% of points wins you 63% of five-setters; 55% wins 95%. Favorites win tennis for a reason.
